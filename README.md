# Algebra 2 study site

Study loop pages for Imagine IM Algebra 2, Unit 2: Section B (working with polynomials, lessons 5 to 7) and Section C (graphs of polynomials, lessons 8 to 11), plus a review page with both.

Open it here: https://cosmeticaura.github.io/algebra-2/

Each section: read the notes, take a short break, practice with flashcards or multiple choice, brain dump with the notes closed, then mark what you missed. Your progress saves in your own browser.
